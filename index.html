Research Focus

Reduced models for high-dimensional physics.

My work sits at the intersection of turbulence, numerical methods, machine learning, and applied mathematics. The goal is to preserve the physical structure that matters while lowering the cost of prediction and analysis.

MZ

Mori-Zwanzig modeling

Learning finite-memory closures and reduced representations for particle dynamics, boundary-layer transition, and other systems where unresolved scales shape observable behavior.

SPH

Lagrangian turbulence

Using particle-based descriptions, smoothed particle hydrodynamics, and large-eddy simulation ideas to study turbulence from a physically interpretable viewpoint.

ML

Scientific machine learning

Building physics-informed neural models, differentiable programs, and data-driven methods that respect conservation, topology, and multiscale structure.
